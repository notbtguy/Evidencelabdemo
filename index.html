Meet the founder

Anukriti Sharma, MBBS · Co-Founder, Evidence Lab · Delhi, India

Anukriti co-founded Evidence Lab with one goal: to get medical students and doctors doing research the right way. She mentors participants one-on-one alongside Sai Prasad, turning clinical curiosity into real submissions and co-authorships.

She is a clinician herself, aiming for Match 2028, so she knows what it takes to fit research around a medical career.
